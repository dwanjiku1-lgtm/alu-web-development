Questions and answers

Where are the campuses?

ALU has campuses in Rwanda and Mauritius.

What does mission, not major mean?

Students choose a mission they care about and build their learning around it.

Who founded ALU?

ALU was founded by Ghanaian educator Fred Swaniker.

Is ALU accredited?

Yes. The Rwanda and Mauritius entities are each accredited in their own country.
